Section 1 - Please describe in 2-3 sentences your methodology for choosing the number of hidden layers, 
            batch size, learning rates, and number of nodes. Please mention any reduction schemas utilized 
            or any noteworthy trial and error experiments.

I chose the 3 hidden layers with sizes of 400, 300, and 200 so that the layers funnel down to a decreasing
size to the final output layer. The learning rate was chosen to begin at 0.15 and after every epoch, the 
learning rate was decayed by a factor of 0.35, where the minimum learning rate was set to be 0.001. This 
was done so that it can have higher learning rate initially and when it reaches the desired accuracy, the
learning rate will decrease over time to minimize fluctuation in the performance of the model. The layer
size and the learning rate was chosen with trial and error. It was found that a single layer was under
performing and struggled to reach accuracy of 98% in 5 epochs, so additional layers was chosen. Initial
learning rate greater than 0.15 were too high and it would fluctuate even at initial stages of learning. 
The decay rate was set as 0.35 as anything higher was decaying too slowly and the performance fluctuated 
while anything lower, the learning rate decayed too quickly and the learning process was too slow. The 
batch size of 25 was chosen at at random and it worked. I chose this as it was a small enough batch that 
is manageable computationally and is not too small to be able to give the model correct direction to learn.
